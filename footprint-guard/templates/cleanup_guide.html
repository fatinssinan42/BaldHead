Digital Footprint Cleanup Guide

Step-by-step instructions to secure your online presence and remove exposed data

📱 Social Media Account Management

1. Audit Your Accounts

  • Make a list of all accounts found in your scan
  • Identify which accounts you still use regularly
  • Note accounts with personal information exposure

2. Delete Unused Accounts

Platform-specific deletion links:

  • GitHub Account Deletion
  • Twitter Account Deactivation
  • Instagram Account Deletion
  • Facebook Account Deletion
  • LinkedIn Account Closure
  • Reddit Account Deactivation

3. Strengthen Privacy Settings

For accounts you want to keep:

  • Set profiles to private/friends-only
  • Remove personal information (phone, address, birthdate)
  • Disable location tagging
  • Review tagged photos and posts
  • Limit who can see your friends list

📧 Email Security

1. Check for Breaches

  • Visit Have I Been Pwned
  • Enter your email addresses
  • Note which services were compromised

2. Change Compromised Passwords

  • Immediately change passwords for breached accounts
  • Use unique passwords for each account
  • Consider using a password manager (Bitwarden, 1Password, LastPass)

3. Enable Two-Factor Authentication

Enable 2FA on all important accounts:

  • Email providers (Gmail, Outlook, etc.)
  • Social media accounts
  • Financial services
  • Cloud storage accounts

🌐 Domain & Website Privacy

1. Enable WHOIS Privacy

  • Contact your domain registrar
  • Request WHOIS privacy protection
  • This hides your personal information from public domain lookups

2. Remove Personal Information from Websites

  • Contact website administrators
  • Request removal under GDPR/CCPA if applicable
  • Use services like DeleteMe or Optery for automated removal

3. Secure DNS Records

  • Review DNS records for sensitive information
  • Remove TXT records with verification codes after use
  • Ensure MX records point to secure mail servers

🔒 Ongoing Protection

1. Set Up Monitoring

  • Create Google Alerts for your name
  • Monitor Have I Been Pwned regularly
  • Check credit reports annually

2. Practice Good Digital Hygiene

  • Use unique usernames across platforms
  • Avoid using personal information in usernames
  • Think before posting personal information
  • Regularly review app permissions

3. Data Broker Opt-Out

Remove your information from data broker sites:

  • Acxiom: optout.acxiom.com
  • Experian: experian.com/optout
  • Equifax: equifax.com/personal/credit-report-services/credit-report-lock-and-alert/
  • TransUnion: transunion.com/credit-help/general-information/opt-out-transunion
